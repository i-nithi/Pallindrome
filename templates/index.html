A Computer Science Engineering student building software where clean web engineering meets practical machine intelligence.

My work sits at the intersection of structured frontend craft and Python-driven backend systems. Rather than treating interfaces and intelligence as separate worlds, I build end-to-end applications — from semantic matching in LostMatch AI and NLP workflows in ElevaTr to responsive platforms like UniGigs and Paceline.

I like understanding how things work beneath abstractions: writing custom layouts without relying on heavy visual templates, modeling data cleanly in SQL, and experimenting with new tools whenever a problem calls for a sharper approach.
